As AI agents become more capable and can help individuals work faster, the next milestone is surfacing: How do you design AI for optimal team collaboration?As a user experience researcher, I decided to go looking for answers in the competitive landscape. Most tools I examined are doing at least one thing well for teams, but very few are thinking holistically about how to connect teams across the full arc of their work, and even fewer are connecting them across the software development and delivery lifecycle.
