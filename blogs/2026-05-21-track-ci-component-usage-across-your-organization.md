If your platform team publishes standardized pipeline components, you've probably encountered this: once they're out in the wild, you lose visibility. You can't see if anyone’s actually using it, who's on which version, or which projects are still running outdated versions that open your organization up to security risks. Now with GitLab 19.0's new Components Analytics view in the CI/CD Catalog, your team gets visibility and important adoption data about how CI/CD components are being utilized across the organization.
