GitLab marks its fourth consecutive year as a Leader in the Gartner Magic Quadrant for DevSecOps Platforms, positioning itself as a control layer for the agentic era with unified governance across security scanning, deployment, and agent management. The post notes GitLab's 99.9% SLA commitment with service credits and announces five platform innovations: next-gen SCM, GitLab Orbit context graph, agent governance, agentic trigger coordination, and Flex pricing.
