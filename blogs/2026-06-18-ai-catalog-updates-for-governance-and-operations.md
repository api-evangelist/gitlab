GitLab 19.1 enables event-driven Duo Flows with triggers for merge conflicts, draft-to-ready transitions, approvals, and work item creation. New governance features include disabling custom agents and flows, restricting catalog content by namespace, upstream flow validation, AI model allowlists, and pattern-based approval that lets developers authorize tools for an entire session.
