GitLab recognizes partner ecosystem excellence across Europe, the Middle East, and Africa. Winners include cc cloud GmbH (Central Europe), Eficode (Northern Europe), Kiratech (Southern Europe), Bynet (Eastern Europe/Israel), Capgemini Sogeti (technical solution), Devoteam (certification), ITDOTCOM (emerging market), Linux Polska (new business), and Conoa (co-marketing).
