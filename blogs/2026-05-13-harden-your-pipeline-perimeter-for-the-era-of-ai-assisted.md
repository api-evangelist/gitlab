AI-assisted development is moving faster than the security models built to govern it — agents write code, open merge requests, and ship changes at a pace where vulnerabilities go unnoticed. The problem isn't a shortage of scanning tools; it's that security lives outside the workflow where decisions actually get made and policies become suggestions. GitLab Ultimate changes that by making application security a core property of the platform itself, not a portal developers have to visit separately.
