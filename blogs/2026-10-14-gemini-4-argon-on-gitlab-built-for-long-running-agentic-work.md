As AI models take on longer, multi-step work, the challenge is putting that power to use across the software lifecycle without adding risk or cost. Teams need to be able to move faster, within enterprise controls and guardrails. Google's Gemini 4 Argon, now available on GitLab Duo Agent Platform , was built for that kind of work.
