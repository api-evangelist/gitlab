GitLab Dedicated for Government has achieved GovRAMP Authorization, removing a critical procurement barrier for agencies ready to modernize their software supply chain. Our single-tenant solution delivers enterprise DevSecOps with enhanced data residency, isolation, and private networking capabilities to meet the most stringent compliance requirements.
