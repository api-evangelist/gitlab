<p>Today, we are excited to announce the release of GitLab 18.10 with <a href="#sast-false-positive-detection-with-gitlab-duo-agent-platform">SAST false positive detection with GitLab Duo Agent Platform</a>, <a href="#purchase-gitlab-credits-on-the-free-tier-on-gitlabcom">credits for free tier users</a>, <a href="#sign-in-securely-with-passkeys">passwordless sign-in with passkeys</a>, <a href="#introducing-the-work-items-list-and-saved-views">work items list and saved views</a>, and much more!</p>
