## Platform

- [Explore the platform](https://about.gitlab.com/platform/): One control plane for humans and AI agents. See how GitLab brings governance, context, and delivery to the entire software development lifecycle.
- [Agentic Orchestration](https://about.gitlab.com/gitlab-duo-agent-platform/): Custom AI workflow automation across the software lifecycle. Specialized agents for planning, coding, security, and deployment with enterprise governance.
- [CI/CD](https://about.gitlab.com/solutions/continuous-integration/): With GitLab’s CI/CD automation solution, teams and their AI agents stay in flow and ship secure software faster while improving code quality.
- [Context Graph](https://about.gitlab.com/gitlab-orbit/): GitLab Orbit is the lifecycle context graph for software engineering, giving every agent first-party context across your code, pipelines, and deployments.
- [Secrets Management](https://about.gitlab.com/gitlab-secrets-manager/): Native secrets management built on OpenBao. Pipeline and runtime secrets in one store, governed by the permissions and audit trail that cover your code.
- [Why GitLab](https://about.gitlab.com/why-gitlab/): Three reasons enterprises choose GitLab: one platform for agentic engineering, unified context across the SDLC, and governance that scales with agent velocity.
